Tao Hong (洪涛)

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Radiology at Boston Children’s Hospital, affiliated with Harvard Medical School, working with Prof. Xiaoqing Wang. My research focuses on developing computational methods for large-scale inverse problems and computational imaging, with an emphasis on model-based and learning-based MRI reconstruction.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Oden Institute, The University of Texas at Austin, with Prof. Umberto Villa, and at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, with Prof. Jeffrey A. Fessler and Prof. Luis Hernandez-Garcia. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Irad Yavneh and Prof. Michael Zibulevsky, where my research focused on numerical optimization and multigrid methods. Ph.D. Thesis.

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of applied mathematics and computational imaging. I develop computational methods that integrate mathematical structure, imaging physics, and data-driven learning to solve large-scale inverse problems.

Numerical Methods

Exploiting mathematical structure for efficient large-scale computation.
Physics + Learning for Computational Imaging

Combining physical models with data-driven learning for reliable reconstruction.
Biomedical & Scientific Imaging

Extracting meaningful information from complex measurements.
